Understanding the Problem

What is Maritime Freight Brokerage?

A customer tells a freight broker: "I want to ship 10 containers of electronics from Chennai to Rotterdam." The broker needs to determine multiple factors:

🚢
Route Selection
Which shipping route should be used? Are there alternatives? Which route is better?
⏱️
Transit & Distance
How long will it take? How far is the route? What are the transshipment points?
💰
Freight Cost
What will the freight cost? What surcharges apply? What profit margin can the broker make?
⚠️
Risk Assessment
What risks exist on this route? How do weather, port congestion, and demand affect pricing?

Our project brings these decisions into one AI-powered platform.

02 / 10
Core Concept

What is an Agent?

An agent is not simply a chatbot. An agent is a software component that has a goal and performs actions to achieve that goal.

  • Receives a shipment request from the user
  • Looks at available routes in the dataset
  • Evaluates each route using scoring criteria
  • Calculates weighted scores across multiple factors
  • Compares alternatives and ranks them
  • Selects the best route and explains why

That is why we call it a Route Agent.

Decision Logic

Route Scoring Engine

The Route Agent doesn't look at just one factor. It uses weighted multi-factor scoring to find the best route.

🕐 Transit Time Score
40%
Faster routes get higher scores. Normalized using min-max scaling.
💰 Cost Score
30%
Cheaper routes get higher scores. Normalized using min-max scaling.
🔄 Transshipment Score
30%
Fewer transshipments = higher score. Each adds a 15-point penalty.
🧮 Scoring Formula
# Overall route score Route Score = 100 - ( transit_norm × 40 + cost_norm × 30 + transshipment_penalty × 30 ) # Example: Chennai → Rotterdam # R001: 24d, $1850, 1 tranship → Score 65.5 # R002: 27d, $1720, 2 tranship → Score 51.0 # R003: 26d, $1780, 1 tranship → Score 55.4 Winner: R001 ✅

Pricing Intelligence

Pricing Agent & Quotation Service

The Pricing Agent adds surcharges and demand adjustments. The Quotation Service combines both agents.
